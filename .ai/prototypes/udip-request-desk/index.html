Wnioski UDIP (Bydgoszcz City Hall) — screen inventory

A staff-only assistant for Biuro Komunikacji Społecznej that registers public-information requests, finds similar past cases, proposes which departments to ask, assembles and anonymises the answer, and tracks the statutory deadline. Interactive backend screens derived from .ai/prototypes/udip-request-desk.requirements.md. The shell, DataTable chrome and CrudForm layout mirror packages/ui/src/backend/; colours come from globals.css. The demo date is Monday, 23 November 2026. All names, addresses and figures are fictional. Department names are illustrative until the official BIP structure is imported.

Main walkthrough: 1 Calendar → 4 Upload & OCR → 5 Request data → 6 Similar requests → 7 Routing proposal → 8 Department note → 9 Case → 10 Department reply → 11 Draft → 12 Anonymisation → 13 Export & record sending. The system never sends anything — every outgoing step is "copy / download, then record what you did".
